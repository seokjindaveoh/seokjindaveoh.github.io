About

I am a first-year Ph.D. student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Texas A&M University. I received my B.S. and M.S. in electrical engineering from Kookmin University, where I worked with Prof. Kyeong-Sik Min on memristor neural networks for Equilibrium Propagation learning.

My work centers on on-device AI hardware: memristor crossbars, local learning algorithms such as Equilibrium Propagation, and hardware–algorithm co-design for power, performance, and area. I want neuromorphic technology to make intelligence efficient enough to run anywhere, including places with limited power.
